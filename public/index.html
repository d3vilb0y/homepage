01 About

I'm a cybersecurity engineer based in Jönköping, Sweden. My work covers security architecture and defence in depth — from the edge (F5, WAF, DDoS, bot protection), through Zero Trust access with Zscaler, down to firewall policy on Check Point and switching on Cisco.

On the systems side: Linux, Windows Server, and Veeam. If it needs to stay up and stay secure, that's my territory. I'm drawn to problems where the failure mode is both a security event and a business event at the same time — and I'd rather design a layer out than firefight one in.

Off-keyboard, I'm building a life in Jönköping. House on the way, wife, one kid, and two cats with strong opinions about unattended keyboards.

02 Work

  1. Security Architecture

    Designing layered controls across edge, network, identity, endpoint, and data — with identity and device posture as the decision plane at every layer. Threat modelling, control mapping, and the unglamorous work of making sure no single control is load-bearing.

    Design · Defence in depth · Threat modelling

  2. Zero Trust & Zscaler

    Identity- and posture-driven access at every layer with Zscaler ZIA and ZPA — replacing flat VPNs with per-app policy gated on who you are and the state of your device. Never trust, always verify.

    ZTNA · SSE · SASE

  3. F5 & Load Balancing

    Application delivery, load balancing, SSL offload, and iRules. Distributing traffic across pools without anyone noticing — the best kind of infrastructure.

    BIG-IP · LTM · GTM

  4. WAF & Bot Protection

    Policy-based Layer 7 defence — blocking injections, scraping, credential stuffing, and automated threats before they reach the application.

    F5 ASM · AWAF · Cloudflare

  5. DDoS Mitigation

    Volumetric and application-layer attack defence. Rate limiting, traffic scrubbing, and behavioural analysis — keeping services alive when things get loud.

    F5 AFM · Upstream scrubbing

  6. Firewalls & Switching

    Check Point gateway policy, VPN, and IPS. Cisco switching and firewall configuration. Network segmentation that actually means something.

    Check Point · Cisco IOS

  7. Systems & Recovery

    Linux and Windows Server administration, hardening, and automation. Veeam-based backup strategy with recovery testing — because a backup you haven't tested is just a false sense of security.

    RHEL · Debian · WinServer · Veeam B&R
