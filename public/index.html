// bio

I'm a cybersecurity engineer based in Jönköping, Sweden. My world covers enterprise WAF, DDoS mitigation, bot protection and load balancing on F5, firewall policy on Check Point, and switching on Cisco — the full perimeter stack.

On the systems side: Linux admin, Windows Server, and Veeam backup. If it needs to stay up and stay secure, that's my territory. I'm drawn to problems where the failure mode is both a security event and a business event at the same time.

Off the clock I'm building a life in Jönköping — house on the way, wife, one kid, and two cats with very strong opinions about unattended keyboards.
